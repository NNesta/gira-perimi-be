## Ibyubahirizwa mbere y’ibindi

Iyo ibimenyetso bitandukanye bitavuga kimwe, ukurikiza iki gikurikiranye:

1. **Ibitegekwa n’umukozi ubifitiye ububasha** (umupolisi);
2. **Ibimenyetso bimurika** (amatara);
3. **Ibyapa** bigenga gutambuka mbere n’**ibimenyetso byo mu muhanda**;
4. **Amategeko rusange** yo gutambuka mbere.

> [!law] Itegeko n° 014/2026, ingingo ya 6
> Amabwiriza atanzwe n’umugenzacyaha cyangwa umukozi ubifitiye ububasha asimbura muri uwo mwanya ibimenyetso bigenga ikoreshwa ry’umuhanda.

## Ibimenyetso by’umukozi ubifitiye ububasha

Umugenzi wese uri mu nzira nyabagendwa agomba guhita yumvira ibitegetswe n’abakozi babifitiye ububasha:

- **Ukuboko kuzamuye**: gutegeka abagenzi bose guhagarara, keretse abamaze kugera mu isangano bagomba guhita bahava.
- **Ukuboko cyangwa amaboko atambitse**: ategeka guhagarara abaturuka mu byerekezo bisanganya icyerekezo cyerekanwa n’ukuboko cyangwa amaboko arambuye.
- **Kuzunguza intambike itara ritukura**: bitegeka guhagarara abo iryo tara riganishaho.
- Umuyobozi w’ikinyabiziga gihagaze ategetswe kugikuraho igihe abisabwe n’umukozi ubifitiye ububasha.

## Amatara y’amabara atatu

| Itara | Icyo rivuga |
|---|---|
| Ritukura | Birabujijwe kurenga icyo kimenyetso. |
| Iry’umuhondo | Birabujijwe gutambuka umurongo wo guhagarara (cyangwa ikimenyetso ubwacyo iyo umurongo udahari), keretse iyo ryatse ukiri hafi cyane ku buryo utagishobora guhagarara mu buryo butagutera ibyago. |
| Iry’icyatsi | Uburenganzira bwo kurenga icyo kimenyetso. |

> [!warning] Icyitonderwa
> Iyo itara ry’umuhondo ryatse warenze umurongo wo guhagarara uri mu masangano, wambukiranya amasangano gusa iyo utateza abandi ibyago.

### Uko amatara akurikirana

- Itara ry’umuhondo ryaka nyuma y’itara ry’icyatsi.
- Itara ritukura ryaka nyuma y’itara ry’umuhondo.
- Itara ry’icyatsi ryaka nyuma y’itara ritukura.

Itara ritukura rishyirwa hejuru, iry’umuhondo hagati, iry’icyatsi hasi. Amatara y’inyongera y’uturangacyerekezo tw’icyatsi ashyirwa munsi cyangwa iruhande rw’itara ry’icyatsi.

### Uturangacyerekezo n’amatara y’abanyamaguru

- Akarangacyerekezo kaka gasobanura kimwe n’itara, ariko icyo kabuza cyangwa gatangira uburenganzira kireba gusa icyerekezo kerekana.
- Iyo akarangacyerekezo k’icyatsi gacanye hamwe n’itara ritukura cyangwa iry’umuhondo, ushobora gukomeza mu cyerekezo kerekana, ariko ukareka abandi bayobozi n’abanyamaguru bagahita.
- Itara ririmo ishusho y’umunyamaguru cyangwa y’igare rireba gusa abanyamaguru cyangwa abatwara amagare na velomoteri.

## Iyo amatara adakora cyangwa amyatsa

- Iyo ikimenyetso kimurika kidakora, ukurikiza itegeko rusange: **gutambuka mbere kw’ibiturutse iburyo**, keretse iyo hari icyapa kigena ugomba gutambuka mbere.
- **Itara ry’umuhondo rimyatsa** rikoreshwa kwerekana ahantu habi cyane: ritanga uburenganzira bwo gutambuka, ariko ukarushaho kwitonda. Ntirihindura amategeko yo gutambuka mbere.
- Amatara ari hejuru y’ibisate by’umuhanda: **itara ritukura rimeze nk’umusaraba** rivuga ko kugenda muri icyo gisate bibujijwe; **akarangacyerekezo k’icyatsi kareba hasi** kavuga ko byemewe.

## Ibikoresho ngarurarumuri

Imbibi ziri ku mpera z’ubwihugiko bw’abanyamaguru zisigwa irangi ry’umuhondo ngarurarumuri. Ku nkengero z’inzira, ubona iburyo bwawe ibikoresho bitukura cyangwa bisa n’icunga rihishije, naho ibumoso bwawe ibyera.

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Umupolisi aruta amatara, amatara aruta ibyapa.
> - Ukuboko kuzamuye: buri wese arahagarara, uretse uri mu isangano ugomba kurivamo.
> - Umuhondo = hagarara, keretse iyo uri hafi cyane ku buryo utahagarara nta byago.
> - Amatara adakora: ukurikiza gutambuka mbere kw’ibiturutse iburyo.
