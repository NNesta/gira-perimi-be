## Ibyiciro by’ibimenyetso

Ibimenyetso bigenga uburyo bwo kugenda mu muhanda birimo ibyiciro bitatu:

1. **Ibyapa**;
2. **Ibimenyetso bimurika** (amatara);
3. **Ibimenyetso byo mu muhanda** (imirongo n’ibishushanyo biri mu muhanda).

Ibyapa byo ku muhanda na byo bigabanyijemo: ibyapa biburira n’ibyo gutambuka mbere, ibyapa bibuza cyangwa bitegeka, n’ibyapa biyobora.

> [!law] Ingingo ya 91
> Abagendera mu nzira nyabagendwa bagomba kubahiriza ibitegekwa n’ibyapa, ibimenyetso bimurika n’ibimenyetso byo mu muhanda, n’iyo bisa n’aho binyuranye n’andi mategeko agenga ibigendera mu muhanda.

## Menya icyapa ukireba ishusho n’ibara

| Ishusho n’ibara | Ubwoko | Icyo bivuga |
|---|---|---|
| Mpandeshatu ifite umuzenguruko utukura | Biburira (A) | Imbere hari icyago: itonde |
| Mpandeshatu icuramye, STOP, mpandenye y’umuhondo | Gutambuka mbere (B) | Ugomba gutanga inzira cyangwa urayihawe |
| Uruziga rufite umuzenguruko utukura | Bibuza (C) | Hari ikintu ubujijwe |
| Uruziga rw’ubururu | Bitegeka (D) | Hari ikintu utegetswe |
| Urukiramende rw’ubururu | Biyobora (E) | Amakuru n’icyerekezo |
| Urukiramende rwera rufite umuzenguruko wirabura | Nyongera (F) | Byuzuza icyapa kiri hejuru yabyo |

## Ibyapa biburira

Ibyapa biburira bibereyeho kumenyesha umugenzi ko hari icyago, bikerekana imiterere yacyo kandi bikamutegeka ubwitonzi budasanzwe. Bimeze nka mpandeshatu ndinganire ifite ubuso bwera buzengurutswe n’ibara ritukura.

::signs A.1a A.1b A.2 A.3 A.4a A.7 A.10 A.11 A.15 A.16 A.25 A.29

### Aho bishyirwa

- Ahatari mu nsisiro, bishyirwa mu ntera iri hagati ya **metero 150 na 200** mbere y’ahantu habi byerekana.
- Iyo iyo ntera iri munsi ya metero 150, yerekanwa n’akapa nyongera **F.1**.
- Mu nsisiro, ibyapa biburira bishyirwa iruhande neza rw’ahantu habi.
- Ku masangano na gariyamoshi, utwapa A.28 dushyirwa muri metero 150, 100 na 50 mbere yayo.

::signs A.26 A.27 A.28a A.28b A.28c F.1

## Ibyapa byo gutambuka mbere

Bimenyesha abagenzi amategeko yihariye yo gutambuka mbere mu nkomane cyangwa mu mfunganwa.

::signs B.1 B.2a B.3 B.4 B.5 B.6

- **B.1 (Tanga inzira)**: ureka ibinyabiziga bigenda mu muhanda wegera bigatambuka.
- **B.2a (STOP)**: ugomba guhagarara akanya gato ku murongo wo guhagarara, hanyuma ugatanga inzira.
- **B.3**: uri mu muhanda utambukwamo mbere; **B.4** yerekana aho bigarukira.
- **B.5**: uha inzira ibinyabiziga biturutse imbere mu mfunganwa; **B.6**: ni wowe utambuka mbere y’ibiturutse imbere.

## Ibyapa bibuza

Bimeze nk’uruziga ruzengurutswe n’ibara ritukura, rufite ubuso bwera. Ibyapa bibuza guhagarara bifite ubuso bw’ubururu.

::signs C.1 C.2a C.3a C.3b C.11a C.12 C.13a C.14 C.15 C.18 C.19 C.17a

- Ibyapa bibuza n’ibitegeka bikurikizwa gusa mu gice cy’inzira kiri hagati y’aho bishinze n’**inkomane ikurikiyeho**, keretse iyo hari akapa nyongera kabivuga ukundi.
- Ku ngasire imwe hashobora gushyirwa ibibuzwa byinshi, ariko ibigereranyo ntibishobora kurenga **bitatu**.
- Icyapa C.17a, C.17b na C.17c byerekana iherezo ry’ibibuzwa.

## Ibyapa bitegeka

Bimeze nk’uruziga rw’ubururu kandi bishyirwa ahantu bibonwa neza n’abo bigenewe.

::signs D.1a D.2 D.3 D.4 D.5 D.8

## Ibyapa biyobora

Biyobora kandi bigasobanurira abagenzi: byerekana ibyerekezo, insisiro, inzira y’icyerekezo kimwe, ibitaro, parikingi n’ibindi.

::signs E.9 E.10 E.11 E.12 E.13a E.14 E.19 E.20

## Aho ibyapa bishingwa

- Ibyapa bishingwa ku ruhande rw’**iburyo** bw’umuhanda.
- Umusozo wo hasi w’icyapa ntushobora kuba munsi ya **metero 1,50** cyangwa hejuru ya **metero 2,10** uhereye ku butaka, uretse ibyapa by’agateganyo.
- Igisobanuro cy’icyapa gishobora kuzuzwa n’ibyapa nyongera by’urukiramende, bishyirwa munsi yacyo.

> [!tip] Inama
> Fungura urutonde rwose rw’ibyapa (ibyapa birenga 150 biri mu iteka) maze wikoreshe amakarita yo kwibuka: urebe icyapa, ubanze utekereze igisobanuro mbere yo kugihindura.

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Mpandeshatu itukura = kuburira; uruziga rutukura = kubuza; uruziga rw’ubururu = gutegeka; urukiramende rw’ubururu = kuyobora.
> - Ahatari mu nsisiro, icyapa kiburira gishyirwa muri metero 150–200 mbere y’icyago.
> - Ibibuzwa bikurikizwa kugeza ku nkomane ikurikiyeho.
> - Icyapa gishingwa iburyo, hagati ya m 1,50 na m 2,10 uvuye ku butaka.
