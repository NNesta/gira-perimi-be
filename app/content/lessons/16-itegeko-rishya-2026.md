## Iri tegeko rigamije iki?

Itegeko n° 014/2026 rigenga ikoreshwa ry’umuhanda **hagamijwe gukumira impanuka zo mu muhanda**. Ryatangiye gukurikizwa ku munsi ryatangarijweho mu Igazeti ya Leta (10/03/2026), kandi ryavanyeho Itegeko n° 34/1987 ryo ku wa 17/09/1987.

> [!info] Isano n’ikizamini
> Ibibazo by’ikizamini cy’uruhushya rw’agateganyo bishingiye cyane cyane ku mabwiriza agenga uburyo bwo kugenda mu muhanda (ibyapa, gutambuka mbere, umuvuduko...). Iri tegeko rishya ryo ryibanda ku mutekano, ku bihano no ku micungire y’impushya. Ni byiza kurimenya nk’umuyobozi w’ejo.

## Ibyo ugomba kumenya

- **Uruhande rugenderwamo** n’ikinyabiziga mu muhanda ni urw’**iburyo**.
- **Amabwiriza y’umukozi ubifitiye ububasha** asimbura ibimenyetso bigenga ikoreshwa ry’umuhanda.
- Ikinyabiziga cyandikwa mu Rwanda ni **icyo icyuma kiyobora kiri ibumoso**, uretse bimwe byavuzwe n’itegeko (nk’imodoka z’abafite ubumuga, amakamyo ya toni 20 kuzamura, bisi zambukiranya imipaka...).
- Imodoka yanditswe mu kindi gihugu ifite icyuma kiyobora iburyo ishobora gukoresha umuhanda w’u Rwanda mu gihe kitarenze **amezi atatu**.

## Arukoro n’ibiyobyabwenge

> [!law] Ingingo ya 9
> Igipimo ntarengwa cya arukoro mu maraso ku muyobozi w’ikinyabiziga ni **garama 0,80 muri litiro imwe y’amaraso**. Iteka rya Minisitiri rishobora kugabanya icyo gipimo.

- Umugenzacyaha cyangwa umukozi ubifitiye ububasha ashobora gupima umuyobozi arukoro n’**ibiyobyabwenge** igihe cyose bibaye ngombwa.
- Utemeye ibyavuye mu ipimwa ashobora gusaba ko ripimwa na **laboratwari yemewe**.

## Kubuza ikinyabiziga kugenda no kugifunga

- **Kubuza ikinyabiziga kugenda** ntibirenza **amasaha 12**. Bikorwa nk’iyo umuyobozi adafite uruhushya, ikinyabiziga kidafite ubwishingizi cyangwa isuzuma rya tekiniki, kivubura imyotsi irenze igipimo, gitwaye ibirenze ibyo kigomba gutwara, n’ibindi.
- **Gufunga ikinyabiziga** ntibirenza **amezi atatu**. Bikorwa nk’iyo cyatawe mu muhanda, cyateje impanuka yahitanye umuntu, nyiracyo atishyuye ihazabu ku gihe, cyangwa umuyobozi wacyo yasanzwemo arukoro irenze igipimo.
- Iyo igihe kirangiye nyiracyo atagikuyeyo, ikinyabiziga **kigurishwa muri cyamunara** cyangwa kigashwanyagurizwa ahabigenewe.

## Amanota y’imyitwarire

Hashyizweho **uburyo bw’ikoranabuhanga bwo gukurikirana imyitwarire** y’umuyobozi ufite uruhushya, hakoreshejwe **amanota y’imyitwarire** ku mikoreshereze y’umuhanda. Iteka rya Minisitiri rigena uko ayo manota abarwa.

## Amande yo mu rwego rw’ubutegetsi

- Uwahanishijwe ihazabu yishyura mu gihe kitarenze **iminsi 30** uhereye igihe yamenyeshejwe ikosa.
- Iyo atishyuye, hiyongeraho **30%** by’ihazabu, yishyurwa mu yindi minsi 30.
- Iyo atabyubahirije, ikinyabiziga cyakoreshejwe **kirafatwa kigafungwa**.
- Utemeye ikosa ashobora **gutakamba** mu minsi itatu y’akazi; agasubizwa mu minsi itatu y’akazi. Iyo adasubijwe ku gihe, bifatwa ko afite ukuri.
