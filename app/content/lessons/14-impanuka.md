## Inshingano zawe ako kanya

Umugenzi wese uguweho n’impanuka agomba:

1. **Guhita ahagarara** igihe bimushobokeye, atabangamiye bundi bushya uburyo bwo kugenda mu muhanda cyangwa ngo yiteze ibyago.
2. **Kurinda aho impanuka yabereye** kugira ngo uburyo bwo kugenda mu muhanda budahungabana: ashyira **mpandeshatu iburira** ahabaye inkomyi, acana icyarimwe **amatara ndangacyerekezo** yose, cyangwa atereka **itara ry’umuhondo rimyatsa**.
3. **Gutabaza** abashinzwe umutekano.

> [!tip] Nimero y’ubutabazi
> Mu gihe cy’impanuka mu muhanda, hamagara **112**.

::signs A.29 A.30 F.6

## Iyo hari uwapfuye cyangwa wakomeretse bikabije

- **Ntuhindure** uko ibintu bimeze aho impanuka yabereye.
- **Ntusibanganye** ibimenyetso bishobora kugaragaza abari mu makosa.
- **Menyesha** abashinzwe umutekano bari hafi aho.
- **Guma aho** impanuka yabereye cyangwa uhagaruke kugeza abashinzwe umutekano bahageze, keretse iyo bakwemereye kuhava, ugiye gutabara abakomeretse cyangwa kwivuza.
- Iyo abandi bahuye n’impanuka babigusabye, **ubabwira umwirondoro wawe**.

> [!info] Gukomereka bikabije cyangwa byoroheje
> - **Uwakomeretse cyane**: ufite ibikomere bishobora kumuviramo urupfu, kumushegesha wese, kumutesha igice cy’umubiri cyangwa wacitse urugingo.
> - **Igikomere cyoroheje**: kitahungabanya ubuzima, kitamushegesha wese, kandi kitatuma atakaza igice cy’umubiri cyangwa urugingo.

## Iyo impanuka yangije ibintu gusa

Iyo impanuka yangije ibintu gusa cyangwa hari abakomeretse byoroheje, abayigizemo uruhare bagaragaza uko byagenze babyumvikanyeho. Iyo batabyumvikanyeho, bahamagara umukozi ubishinzwe.

## Ikinyabiziga cyaguye mu muhanda

Iyo ikinyabiziga gihagaze kubera impanuka, cyangwa ibyo gitwaye bikagwa mu nzira nyabagendwa, umuyobozi wacyo akora uko ashoboye kugira ngo inzira igendwemo nta nkomyi. Iyo atabishoboye, umuherekeje ni we ubikora; yaba ntawe, bigakorwa n’undi mugenzi waguweho n’iyo mpanuka.

## Icyo itegeko rishya rivuga

- **Impanuka yo mu muhanda** ni impanuka itaturutse ku bushake kandi itunguranye, ibera mu muhanda, irimo ikinyabiziga kimwe cyangwa byinshi bigenda, igateza gukomereka, ihungabana, ubumuga cyangwa urupfu, cyangwa kwangirika kw’ibintu.
- **Impanuka yahitanye umuntu** ni iyo nibura umuntu umwe yapfiriyemo aho yabereye cyangwa mu minsi 30 ikurikiye.
- Ikinyabiziga cyateje impanuka yapfiriyemo umuntu **gishobora gufungwa** (Itegeko n° 014/2026, ingingo ya 18).
- Kwanga guhagarara utegetswe n’umugenzacyaha cyangwa umukozi ubifitiye ububasha ni **icyaha**.

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Hagarara, rinda aho impanuka yabereye (mpandeshatu, amatara yo kuburira), hamagara 112.
> - Iyo hari uwapfuye cyangwa wakomeretse bikabije: ntuhindure ibintu, guma aho kugeza abashinzwe umutekano bahageze.
> - Tanga umwirondoro wawe iyo ubisabwe.
