## Uburumbarare ntarengwa

| Igipimo | Ntarengwa |
|---|---|
| Ubugari bw’ikinyabiziga | m 2,65 |
| Ubuhagarike bw’ikinyabiziga | m 4,20 |
| Uburebure: ikinyabiziga gifite umutambiko umwe (cyangwa imitambiko ibiri yegeranye) | m 7 |
| Uburebure: ikinyabiziga gifite imitambiko ibiri cyangwa irenga | m 11 |
| Uburebure bwa makuzungu | m 17,40 |
| Ibinyabiziga bikururana, icya mbere gikururwa n’inyamaswa | m 18 |
| Igice kirenga ku biziga by’inyuma | m 3,50 |
| Igice kirenga ku biziga by’imbere | m 2,70 |

Ubugari bwa romoruki ikuruwe n’igare cyangwa velomoteri ntiburenza **cm 75**; ikuruwe n’ipikipiki idafite akanyabiziga ku ruhande ntiburenza **m 1,25**.

## Imizigo

Imizigo igomba gupakirwa no gupangwa ku buryo idashobora:

- guteza abantu ibyago;
- kwangiza inzira nyabagendwa;
- kubuza umuyobozi kubona neza cyangwa kubangamira imiyoborere;
- gukingiriza amatara, uturebanyuma, ibimenyetso by’icyerekezo, utugarurarumuri cyangwa nimero z’ikinyabiziga.

| Imizigo | Ntarengwa |
|---|---|
| Ubugari bw’ikinyabiziga gipakiye | m 2,50 |
| Ibinyampeke, ikawa, amakara, ubwatsi bidahambiriye | m 2,75 (m 3 mu ntera itarenga km 25, uretse mu nsisiro) |
| Ubuhagarike bw’imizigo | m 4 |
| Imizigo miremire irenga inyuma | m 3 |
| Imizigo y’amagare na velomoteri: ubugari / irenga inyuma | cm 75 / cm 50 |

> [!info] Imizigo irenga inyuma ho metero irenga
> - Ku manywa: igaragazwa n’**agatambaro gatukura** gafite nibura cm 50 z’uruhande.
> - Iyo amatara agomba gucanwa: igaragazwa n’**itara ritukura** cyangwa **akagarurarumuri gatukura**.

## Uburemere bwemewe

- Ku mutambiko usanzwe: **toni 10**.
- Ku mitambiko ibiri ikurikiranye: **toni 16**.
- Ku mitambiko itatu: **toni 24**.
- Ikamyo iyo ari yo yose ntishobora gutwara ibirenze **toni 53**.

Uburemere bwemewe ku iteme cyangwa ku gice cy’umuhanda bushobora kugabanywa, bukerekanwa n’icyapa **C.7**.

## Gukurura no gukururana

- Ikinyabiziga kigendeshwa na moteri n’igikururwa n’inyamaswa ntibishobora gukurura ibinyabiziga **birenze bibiri**. Ipikipiki ifite akanyabiziga ku ruhande ntishobora gukurura romoruki n’imwe.
- Gushyira mu muhanda ibinyabiziga bikururana birenze bitatu bisaba uruhushya rwa Minisitiri.
- Iyo hagati ya romoruki n’ikinyabiziga kiyikurura hari umwanya urenze **metero 3**, ikibizirikanyije kigaragazwa ku manywa n’agatambaro gatukura, nijoro n’ikimenyetso gisa n’icunga rihishije.
- **Ibizirikisho by’iminyururu cyangwa by’insinga** by’ingoboka bikoreshwa gusa nta kundi byagenda, kugira ngo ikinyabiziga kigere aho gikorerwa, kandi nturenze **km 20 mu isaha**. Bigaragazwa n’**icyapa cyera cya mpande enye** gifite nibura cm 30 kuri buri ruhande.

## Feri

Buri modoka igomba kugira:

1. **Feri y’urugendo**: ituma imodoka igenda buhoro kandi igahagarara vuba kandi neza.
2. **Feri yo guhagarara umwanya munini**: ituma imodoka iguma aho iri, n’iyo umuyobozi atarimo, ku muzamuko cyangwa agacuri bya **16%**.
3. **Feri yo gutabara**: ituma imodoka igenda buhoro kandi igahagarara igihe feri y’urugendo idakora neza.

Velomoteri n’ipikipiki bigira ibyuma bibiri byo guhagarara: kimwe gifunga uruziga rw’inyuma, ikindi uruziga rw’imbere.

## Ibindi bikoresho by’ingenzi

- **Akarebanyuma** gatuma umuyobozi abona ibiri inyuma n’ibumoso bwe.
- **Agahanagura-kirahuri** nibura kamwe, n’**akoza-kirahuri**.
- **Impombo y’imyotsi** ifite akagabanya-rusaku; mu nsisiro birabujijwe gusakurisha moteri ikinyabiziga gihagaze.
- **Inziga**: amano afite ubujyakuzimu butari munsi ya **milimetero imwe**.
- **Icyerekana umuvuduko** ku kinyabiziga gishobora kurenza km 40 mu isaha.
- **Imikandara** ku ntebe z’imbere (umuyobozi n’umugenzi wicaye imbere).
- **Mpandeshatu iburira** ifite nibura cm 40 kuri buri ruhande.
- **Agasanduku k’ubutabazi bw’ibanze** (ibipfuko, umuti wica mikorobi).
- Ikintu kibuza imodoka kwibwa.

> [!warning] Birabujijwe
> Uretse igihe gikuruwe n’ikindi, nta kinyabiziga gifite moteri gishobora kumanuka ahamanuka **moteri itaka** cyangwa **vitesi idakora** (point mort).

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Ubugari ntarengwa m 2,65 (m 2,50 hapakiye); ubuhagarike m 4,20 (imizigo m 4).
> - Imizigo irenga inyuma ho m 1: agatambaro gatukura ku manywa, itara ritukura nijoro.
> - Gukurura n’umugozi w’ingoboka: ntarenga km 20 mu isaha, icyapa cyera cya cm 30.
> - Amano y’inziga: nibura mm 1.
