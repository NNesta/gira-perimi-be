## Ibimenyetso byo mu muhanda ni iki?

Ni ibimenyetso bishyirwa mu muhanda cyangwa ku nkengero z’utuyira tw’abanyamaguru, bigenewe gutunganya uburyo bwo kugendera mu muhanda, kuburira cyangwa kuyobora abagenzi. Bishobora gukoreshwa byonyine cyangwa biri kumwe n’ibindi bimenyetso.

## Imirongo irombereje

::signs M.1 M.2 M.3 M.4

- **Umurongo wera udacagaguye**: umuyobozi wese abujijwe kuwurenga. Birabujijwe kandi kugendera ibumoso bwawo iyo utandukanya ibyerekezo byombi by’umuhanda.
- **Umurongo wera ucagaguye**: abujijwe kuwurenga, keretse iyo agomba kunyura ku kindi kinyabiziga, gukatira ibumoso, guhindukira cyangwa kujya mu kindi gisate cy’umuhanda.
- **Uduce tugufi kandi twegeranye cyane**: tuvuga ko umurongo udacagaguye wegereje.
- **Umurongo udacagaguye n’ucagaguye bibangikanye**: umuyobozi yita gusa ku murongo umwegereye. Uwarenze iyo mirongo anyura ku kindi kinyabiziga ashobora kongera kuyirenga asubira mu mwanya we.

> [!tip] Inama
> Uri ku ruhande rw’umurongo ucagaguye, ushobora kunyuranaho niba nta kindi kibibuza. Uri ku ruhande rw’umurongo udacagaguye, ntukawurenge.

## Imirongo yo ku nkengero n’ibisate byihariye

::signs M.5 M.6 M.7 M.8 M.9 M.10

- **Igice gikikijwe n’uduce tugari tw’ibara ryera**: kigenewe ibinyabiziga bigenda buhoro n’ibitwara abantu muri rusange. Bishobora kunyura mu gisate gikurikiyeho bishaka kunyuranaho, bigasubira mu mwanya wabyo bikimara kubinyuraho.
- **Imirongo ibiri yera icagaguye ibangikanye**, ifunganye ku buryo imodoka zitahanyura: ni **agahanda k’amagare**.
- **Umurongo wera udacagaguye ku nkengero nyayo**: werekana aho umuhanda ugarukira.
- **Umurongo w’umuhondo ucagaguye ku nkengero**: guhagarara umwanya munini birabujijwe ku burebure bwawo.
- **Umurongo wera mugari udacagaguye**: werekana inkengero itari nyayo; igice kiri hirya yawo kigenewe guhagararwamo.

## Imirongo yambukiranya umuhanda

- **Umurongo mugari wera udacagaguye** wambukiranya: werekana aho ugomba guhagarara bitewe n’icyapa B.2 (STOP) cyangwa itara.
- **Umurongo ugizwe na mpandeshatu nyinshi zera**, amasonga yazo areba abayobozi: werekana aho uhagarara iyo ari ngombwa kugira ngo utange inzira hakurikijwe icyapa B.1.
- **Imirongo yera migari iteganye n’umurongo ugabanya umuhanda mo kabiri**: ni inzira y’abanyamaguru.
- **Imirongo ibiri icagaguye igizwe na kare cyangwa ingirwamwashi zera**: ni aho abatwara amagare na velomoteri bambukiranya umuhanda.

::signs B.1 B.2a E.11

## Ibindi bimenyetso

- **Uturangacyerekezo twera** hafi y’amasangano twerekana igisate ugomba gukurikira ngo ugane mu cyerekezo kerekanwa.
- **Uturangacyerekezo two gukata** dukurikiye umurongo ucagaguye bimenyesha ko umubare w’ibisate ugiye kugabanuka.
- **Inyandiko z’ibara ryera** mu muhanda zuzuza cyangwa zibutsa ibyerekanwa n’ibyapa.
- **Imirongo iberamye iteganye** yerekana imyanya ibinyabiziga bibujijwe kunyuramo.

## Ibimenyetso by’agateganyo

**Imitemeri y’ibara risa n’icunga rihishije** ishobora gusimbura imirongo yera irombereje, idacagaguye n’icagaguye. Iyo iri aho, **ivanaho agaciro** k’imirongo yera iri ahantu hamwe.

> [!warning] Icyitonderwa
> Ahakorerwa imirimo, ukurikiza imitemeri y’icunga n’ibyapa by’agateganyo, si imirongo yera isanzwe.

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Udacagaguye: ntukawurenge. Ucagaguye: urarenga gusa unyuranaho, ukatira ibumoso, uhindukira cyangwa uhindura igisate.
> - Imirongo ibiri ibangikanye: wita ku ukwegereye.
> - Umuhondo ucagaguye ku nkengero: ntuhahagarare umwanya munini.
> - Imitemeri y’icunga isimbura imirongo yera ahakorerwa imirimo.
