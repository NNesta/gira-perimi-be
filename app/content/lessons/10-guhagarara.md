## Aho ikinyabiziga gihagarara

Uretse ahari amategeko yihariye, ikinyabiziga gihagaze umwanya muto cyangwa munini kigomba kuba:

- **iburyo** hakurikijwe aho cyaganaga; mu muhanda w’icyerekezo kimwe gishobora no kuba ibumoso;
- **hafi y’akayira k’abanyamaguru**, ariko umwanya uri hagati y’ibiziga n’akayira ntube urenga **cm 50**;
- **kure y’umurongo** ugabanya umuhanda mo kabiri, kandi byaba ngombwa ahitaruye umuhanda;
- **gitonze umurongo ubangikanye** n’inkombe y’umuhanda.

Iyo uhagaze ku mwanya wo ku ruhande wagenewe abanyamaguru, ubasigira akanya nibura **metero imwe** y’ubugari.

## Mbere yo kuva mu kinyabiziga

- Ntusiga ikinyabiziga cyangwa inyamaswa zawe utabanje kwiringira ko nta mpanuka iri bube, kandi ko ikinyabiziga kitakoreshwa nta ruhushya rwawe.
- **Ntukingura umuryango** cyangwa ngo uve mu kinyabiziga utabanje kureba ko bidatera ibyago cyangwa ngo bibangamire abandi.

## Ikinyabiziga gihagaze nijoro cyangwa ahabujijwe

Ikinyabiziga gihagaritswe ku muhanda kigomba kurangwa kure n’ikimenyetso kigaragara, cyane cyane:

- iyo gihagaritswe **nijoro** ku buryo abakigana batamenya ko kibabereye imbogamizi;
- iyo umuyobozi yagombye kugihagarika **ahantu habujijwe guhagarara**.

Gishobora kurangwa na **mpandeshatu iburira**, n’**amatara ndangacyerekezo yose acanye icyarimwe** (warning), cyangwa n’itara ry’umuhondo rimyatsa.

> [!info] Intera hagati y’ibinyabiziga bihagaze
> Ku muhanda ubisikanirwamo utuma hahita ibinyabiziga bibiri gusa, hagati y’ibinyabiziga bibiri bihagaze ku ruhande rumwe hasigara nibura **metero 5** mu nsisiro na **metero 20** ahatari mu nsisiro.

## Aho guhagarara umwanya muto n’umunini bibujijwe

- Ahantu hose bigaragara ko hashobora guteza ibyago cyangwa kubangamira abandi;
- mu **duhanda tw’amagare**, mu bice byagenewe abanyamaguru n’abambukiranya, no muri **metero 5** mbere y’aho abanyamaguru bambukira;
- ku **nzira z’abanyamaguru** n’inkengero ziciye hejuru mu nsisiro;
- ahateganye n’ikindi kinyabiziga gihagaze, iyo umuhanda usigara udafite metero 6 (ibyerekezo byombi) cyangwa metero 3 (icyerekezo kimwe);
- **hejuru no munsi y’amateme** no mu mihanda inyura ikuzimu;
- muri **metero 10** uhereye ku nkombe y’umuhanda wambukiranya (amasangano);
- **mu ikoni** cyangwa hafi y’**impinga y’umupando** iyo utabona muri metero 20 mu nsisiro, cyangwa metero 100 ahatari mu nsisiro;
- muri **metero 2** imbere y’icyapa cyangwa itara;
- aho kunyura ku kinyabiziga cyawe byatuma abandi barenga umurongo udacagaguye;
- ahari ibyapa bibibuza.

::signs C.18 C.19 C.20a C.20b

## Aho guhagarara umwanya munini bibujijwe

- Imbere y’ahinjirwa n’ahasohokerwa n’abantu benshi, n’imbere y’amashuri cyangwa ahantu h’imyidagaduro mu masaha bafunguriraho;
- imbere y’aho binjirira mu bibanza, uretse ibinyabiziga by’abahatuye;
- muri **metero 1** imbere cyangwa inyuma y’ikindi kinyabiziga gihagaze;
- muri **metero 15** imbere n’inyuma y’ikimenyetso cy’aho imodoka zitwara abantu zihagarara;
- mu muhanda wo hagati mu nzira igizwe n’imihanda itatu;
- mu gisate kigaragazwa n’imirongo idacagaguye;
- ku ruhande rw’**umurongo w’umuhondo ucagaguye**;
- ahatari mu nsisiro, ku muhanda utambukwamo mbere (B.3);
- iyo umuhanda usigaye utagifite **metero 3** z’ubugari;
- muri **metero 1** uvuye ku ruhombo rw’amazi azimya umuriro.

::signs E.19 E.20 M.8

> [!warning] Ibinyabiziga bimara igihe kirekire
> Birabujijwe gusiga ku nzira nyabagendwa, mu gihe kirenze **iminsi 7**, ikinyabiziga gifite moteri kitagishoboye kugenda cyangwa romoruki. Umukozi ubifitiye ububasha ashobora gukuraho ikinyabiziga cyahagaritswe binyuranyije n’amategeko, nyiracyo akishyura amafaranga yo kugikuraho.

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Hagarara iburyo, ubangikanye n’inkombe, utarenze cm 50 uvuye ku kayira.
> - Ntuhagarare muri m 5 mbere y’inzira y’abanyamaguru, muri m 10 y’amasangano, cyangwa muri m 15 y’aho bisi zihagarara.
> - Nijoro cyangwa ahabujijwe: shyiraho mpandeshatu iburira n’amatara yo kuburira.
> - Reba inyuma mbere yo gukingura umuryango.
