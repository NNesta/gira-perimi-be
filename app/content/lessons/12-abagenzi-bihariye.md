## Abanyamaguru

- Utuyira turi ku mpande z’umuhanda n’inkengero ziciye hejuru bihariwe **abanyamaguru**.
- Iyo nta tuyira duhari, abanyamaguru banyura ku musezero w’umuhanda, batabangamiye ibinyabiziga.
- Abanyamaguru ntibambukiranya umuhanda batabanje kureba ko nta byago; bagomba kunyura mu myanya yabagenewe iyo iri muri **metero 50**.
- Ahari amatara yagenewe abanyamaguru, bubahiriza ibyo ayo matara yerekana.
- Nta na rimwe abanyamaguru batinda cyangwa bahagarara mu muhanda nta mpamvu.
- Abasunika utunyabiziga tw’abana, tw’abarwayi cyangwa tw’ibimuga bubahiriza amategeko y’abanyamaguru.

::signs A.10 A.11 D.5 E.11

## Amagare na velomoteri

Abatwara amagare na velomoteri babujijwe:

- gutwara **badafashe amahembe**, badakandagiye ku birenge, bakurura inyamaswa cyangwa bakururwa;
- gutwara cyangwa gusunika ibintu byabangamira imiyoborere cyangwa byateza ibyago;
- kunyura mu muhanda kandi hari **agahanda k’amagare** kagendeka;
- kurenza umuvuduko wa **km 40 mu isaha** mu kayira k’amagare;
- kunyura hagati y’imirongo ibiri y’ibinyabiziga bibangikanye.

Bashobora kugenda babangikanye mu muhanda, ariko bagenda **bashoreranye** mu nsisiro, nijoro, n’igihe bagiye guhura n’ikinyabiziga gifite moteri cyangwa gikururwa n’inyamaswa.

::signs A.12 D.4 C.3c C.3d

## Amapikipiki na velomoteri: ingofero

> [!law] Ingofero yo kwirinda
> Abayobozi ba velomoteri n’amapikipiki n’ababihetsweho bagomba kwambara **ingofero yo kubarinda** yabigenewe, kandi ifunze ku buryo bufashe.

- Abayobozi ba velomoteri, amapikipiki n’amagare bagomba kuyobora bicaye ku kinyabiziga, kandi bagatwara umuntu hakurikijwe amabwiriza y’uwagikoze.
- Igare, velomoteri n’ipikipiki ntibishobora gutwara abantu barenze umubare wateganyijwe.
- Uwasunika igare cyangwa velomoteri atagitwaye yubahiriza amategeko y’abanyamaguru.

## Amatungo n’inyamaswa

- Umuyobozi w’amatungo afashwa n’umubare uhagije w’abaherekeza: **nibura umuherekeza umwe ku nyamaswa umunani**.
- Imikumbi igabanywamo udutsiko tw’inyamaswa **zitarenze icumi**, dutandukanyijwe na **metero 20**.
- Birabujijwe kurekera amatungo mu nzira nyabagendwa.
- Inyamaswa zikurura ikinyabiziga ntizirenza **enye zikurikiranye** n’**eshatu zibangikanye**.
- Kuva bwije kugeza bukeye, uyobora amatungo atwara itara ryera cyangwa ry’umuhondo.

::signs A.13 A.14 D.7

> [!tip] Inama
> Uhuye n’amatungo mu muhanda, gabanya umuvuduko kandi ntuvuze ihoni cyangwa ngo usakurishe moteri hafi yayo.

## Abagenzi mu kinyabiziga

- Birabujijwe gutwara abantu barenze umubare w’intebe wateganyijwe n’uwakoze ikinyabiziga.
- Birabujijwe gutwara ku ntebe y’imbere **abana badafite imyaka 12** iyo hari indi myanya.
- Birabujijwe gutwara abantu mu bice by’inyuma bya karisori cyangwa bya romoruki.
- Umuyobozi ashobora kwemerera umuntu kwicara mu gice cyagenewe ibintu iyo abona bidashobora kuvamo impanuka kandi umutekano wabo wizewe.

## Imyaka y’ifatizo yo kuyobora

| Uwo ari we | Imyaka |
|---|---|
| Abayobozi b’imodoka zo mu nzego C, D, E na F | 20 |
| Abayobozi b’ibinyabiziga byo mu nzego A na B | 18 |
| Abayobozi ba velomoteri (nta wundi uyiriho) | 15 (17 mu bindi bihe) |
| Abayobora inyamaswa zikurura, zikorera cyangwa zigenderwaho | 14 |
| Abayobora amatungo | 12 |

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Abanyamaguru bambukira mu nzira yabo iyo iri muri m 50.
> - Ingofero ni itegeko ku bayobozi ba moto na velomoteri no ku bo bahetse.
> - Mu kayira k’amagare: ntarenga km 40 mu isaha.
> - Umuherekeza umwe ku nyamaswa 8; udutsiko tw’inyamaswa 10 dutandukanyijwe na m 20.
> - Umwana uri munsi y’imyaka 12 ntiyicara imbere iyo hari indi myanya.
