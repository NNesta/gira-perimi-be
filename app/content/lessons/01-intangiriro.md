## Uruhushya rw’agateganyo ni iki?

Mbere yo kubona uruhushya rwa burundu rwo gutwara ikinyabiziga, ubanza gutsinda **ikizamini cy’amategeko y’umuhanda**. Ugitsinze uhabwa **uruhushya rw’agateganyo** rukwemerera kwiga gutwara, uri kumwe n’umwigisha ubifitiye uburenganzira.

Nk’uko Itegeko n° 014/2026 ribiteganya, urwego rufite kurinda umutekano wo mu muhanda mu nshingano ni rwo rukoresha ibizamini by’impushya zo gutwara ibinyabiziga kandi akaba ari rwo ruzitanga.

> [!law] Itegeko n° 014/2026, ingingo ya 23
> Umuyobozi w’ikinyabiziga utwaye ikinyabiziga kigendeshwa na moteri mu muhanda agomba kugira uruhushya rwo kugitwara.

## Uko ikizamini giteye

| Ibigize ikizamini | Uko bimeze |
|---|---|
| Umubare w’ibibazo | Ibibazo 20 |
| Igihe cyagenwe | Iminota 20 |
| Amanota yo gutsinda | 60% (nibura ibisubizo 12 by’ukuri kuri 20) |
| Uburyo | Kuri mudasobwa cyangwa ku rupapuro |
| Imyaka | Imyaka 16 kuzamura |

Buri kibazo kigira ibisubizo bine (a, b, c, d) kandi kimwe gusa ni cyo cy’ukuri. Iyo iminota 20 irangiye, uburyo bw’ikoranabuhanga burifunga ubwabwo bukakwereka niba watsinze.

> [!tip] Inama
> Ibibazo by’ikizamini bikunze kugaruka ku byapa, ku gutambuka mbere, ku muvuduko no ku matara. Ibyo bice ubyige neza kurusha ibindi.

## Uko wiyandikisha

1. Jya ku rubuga rwa **Irembo** (irembo.gov.rw) cyangwa ku mukozi wa Irembo ukwegereye.
2. Hitamo serivisi yo kwiyandikisha ku kizamini cy’uruhushya rw’agateganyo.
3. Hitamo aho uzakorera ikizamini n’itariki, hanyuma wishyure amafaranga y’ikizamini.
4. Ku munsi w’ikizamini, jyana indangamuntu yawe kandi uhagere kare.

> [!warning] Icyitonderwa
> Gukoresha undi muntu ngo agukorere ikizamini ni icyaha gihanwa n’amategeko. Wiga neza, ugatsinda ku bushobozi bwawe.

## Uko wakwiga ukoresheje Gira Perimi

1. **Iga amasomo** uko akurikiranye. Buri somo rirangirana n’ibyo kwibuka.
2. **Menya ibyapa** ukoresheje urutonde rw’ibyapa n’amakarita yo kwibuka.
3. **Witoze** ibibazo by’ikizamini nyirizina ukurikije insanganyamatsiko, urebe ako kanya igisubizo cy’ukuri.
4. **Kora ibizamini by’igerageza** bimeze nk’ikizamini cya Polisi: ibibazo 20 mu minota 20.
5. **Kurikirana aho ugeze** ku kibaho cyawe: impuzandengo y’amanota yawe n’uko witeguye.

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Ikizamini kigira ibibazo 20 bisubizwa mu minota 20.
> - Ugomba kubona nibura 12 kuri 20 (60%) kugira ngo utsinde.
> - Kuri Gira Perimi, iyo umaze gukora ibizamini by’igerageza birenga 10 kandi impuzandengo yawe ikarenga 15/20, tukugira inama yo kujya gukora ikizamini nyirizina.
