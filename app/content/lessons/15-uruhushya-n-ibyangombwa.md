## Ugomba kugira uruhushya

Ntawe ushobora gutwara ikinyabiziga kigendeshwa na moteri mu nzira nyabagendwa adafite kandi atitwaje **uruhushya rwo gutwara** rwatanzwe n’urwego rubifitiye ububasha. Umuyobozi agomba kwerekana ako kanya uruhushya rwe igihe abisabwe n’umukozi ubifitiye ububasha.

## Inzego z’impushya

| Urwego | Ibinyabiziga |
|---|---|
| A | Amapikipiki n’ibinyamitende itatu bifite moteri, bifite cyangwa bidafite intebe ku ruhande |
| B | Imodoka zitwara abantu zifite imyanya itarenze 8 hatabariwemo uw’umuyobozi, imodoka zitarenza kg 5.000, n’ibinyamitende ine bifite moteri |
| C | Imodoka zitwara ibintu zifite uburemere ntarengwa burenga kg 5.000 |
| D | Imodoka zitwara abantu zifite imyanya irenga 8 hatabariwemo uw’umuyobozi |
| E | Ibinyabiziga bikomatanye bifite romoruki irenga kg 750, igikurura kiri mu rwego B, C cyangwa D |
| F | Ibinyabiziga bidasanzwe (imashini zihinga, izikora imihanda, iziterura...) |

## Uruhushya rw’agateganyo

- Rutangwa iyo **watsinze ikizamini cy’amasomo** (amategeko y’umuhanda).
- Rufite agaciro ku **nzego zose** z’ibinyabiziga.
- Ufite uruhushya rw’agateganyo **ntiyemerewe gutwara imodoka** keretse ari kumwe n’umwigisha ufite uruhushya rwo mu rwego rw’imodoka atwaye, wicaye iruhande rwe.
- Ikinyabiziga cyigirwaho kigira inyuma inyuguti **L** y’ibara ry’ubururu ifite nibura **cm 15**, cyangwa icyapa cyera cyanditseho **AUTO-ECOLE** mu nyuguti z’umukara.

> [!info] Amashuri yigisha gutwara
> Itegeko n° 014/2026 riteganya ko kwigisha gutwara bikorwa n’ufite ishuri ryigisha gutwara wabiherewe uruhushya, kandi urwego rushinzwe umutekano wo mu muhanda ni rwo rugenzura amashuri yigisha gutwara.

## Ubuzima busabwa

Urwego rufite kurinda umutekano wo mu muhanda mu nshingano rushobora kutemerera umuntu gukora ikizamini, cyangwa guhagarika by’agateganyo uruhushya rwe, iyo afite:

- **kutabona guhagije** kutakosowe n’indorerwamo zabugenewe;
- **kutumva** cyangwa kumva buhoro bikabije ku matwi yombi;
- indwara y’umubiri ibuza ku buryo bukabije ingingo z’umubiri kuyega neza;
- uburwayi buhungabanya imikorere y’**ubwonko**.

Uwakize cyangwa wakosowe agaragaza icyemezo cya muganga wemewe na Leta, agasubizwa uburenganzira bwe.

## Guhagarikirwa cyangwa kwamburwa uruhushya

- Uruhushya rushobora **guhagarikwa by’agateganyo** kubera imyitwarire mibi mu muhanda. Icyo gihe ntiwemerewe gutwara mu Rwanda, n’iyo waba ufite uruhushya rwo mu kindi gihugu.
- **Urukiko** ni rwo rutesha agaciro uruhushya, rubisabwe n’urwego rushinzwe umutekano wo mu muhanda. Uwarwambuwe ntiyemerewe kongera gukora ikizamini mbere y’**amezi 12**.

## Ibyangombwa by’ikinyabiziga

- **Isuzuma rya tekiniki**: ikinyabiziga kigendeshwa na moteri kigendera mu muhanda iyo cyasuzumwe n’ikigo cyabiherewe uburenganzira kikagihera icyemezo.
- **Ubwishingizi**: ikinyabiziga kidafite ubwishingizi gishobora kubuzwa kugenda.
- **Ibyapa biranga ikinyabiziga** byemewe n’amategeko.
- Igihe ikinyabiziga kigurishijwe cyangwa gitanzwe, **ihererekanya** ryacyo rigomba kwandikwa.

> [!warning] Ikinyabiziga gishobora kubuzwa kugenda (amasaha 12) iyo
> - umuyobozi wacyo adafite uruhushya rwemewe;
> - kitakorewe isuzuma rya tekiniki ritegetswe;
> - kidafite ubwishingizi;
> - kitagira ibyapa cyangwa gifite ibitemewe;
> - gitwaye ibirenze ibyo kigomba gutwara;
> - gifite ibibazo bya tekiniki bibangamira umutekano.

> [!key] Ibyo kwibuka
> - Urwego B: imodoka zitwara abantu bagera ku 8 n’izitarenza kg 5.000.
> - Ufite uruhushya rw’agateganyo atwara ari kumwe n’umwigisha.
> - Ikinyabiziga cyigirwaho: inyuguti L y’ubururu (cm 15) cyangwa AUTO-ECOLE.
> - Uwambuwe uruhushya n’urukiko ategereza amezi 12 mbere yo kongera gukora ikizamini.
